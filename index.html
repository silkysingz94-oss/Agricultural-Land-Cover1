06 / INTERPRETATION
OpenStreetMap agricultural features are mapped objects, not a complete national agricultural census. Missing mapped farmland does not mean that farmland is absent. ESA WorldCover is a 2021 reference product and should not be presented as a current crop-type classification. Sentinel-1 crop phenology and crop-species classification require a separate time-series processing and validation workflow.
